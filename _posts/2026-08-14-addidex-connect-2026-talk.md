Luis Arturo Pacheco presented Animaquina at Addidex Connect 2026 in Haarlem, the Netherlands. The recording is now online.

The talk asks why industrial robots are still so hard to program, and what changes when you control them directly inside Blender. It demonstrates live robot control, real-time digital twins, procedural constraints, multi-robot workflows, Geometry Nodes toolpaths, modular end effectors, and non-planar and conformal 3D printing.
