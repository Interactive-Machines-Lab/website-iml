"Procedural Animation for Intuitive Industrial Robot Programming", by Luis Arturo Pacheco Alcala, Madeline Gannon and Shahin Vassigh, appears in the proceedings of SIGraDi 2025: Meta Responsive Approaches. The conference took place at the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba, Argentina, on November 19–21, 2025.

The paper links Blender's animation tools to industrial robot control through visual programming, so designers can animate robot motion instead of writing toolpath programs.
