Two Ph.D. researchers at Florida Atlantic University are now listed as affiliated researchers of the Interactive Machines Lab.

[Christine Khouri Sader]({{ '/people/christine-khouri-sader' | relative_url }}) is an architectural designer and Ph.D. student in Comparative Studies. Her research investigates how robotic movement and sound can create spatial experiences, with an emerging focus on performance.

[Smaro Katsangelou]({{ '/people/smaro-katsangelou' | relative_url }}) is an architect and engineer pursuing a Ph.D. on architecture and artificial intelligence. Her work explores the intersection of new technologies and architectural heritage.
