Luis Arturo Pacheco presented "From Pen Plotters to Robots: Geometry Nodes for Parametric Toolpathing" at Blender Conference 2025, held at Felix Meritis in Amsterdam on September 17–19, 2025.

The talk showed how Geometry Nodes can generate parametric toolpaths for pen plotters, laser cutters, 3D printers and industrial robots. It also introduced [Animaquina](https://www.animaquina.com), the open-source add-on for designing, simulating and controlling robot motion inside Blender.
